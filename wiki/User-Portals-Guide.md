# 👥 User Portals & Workflow Guide

BhoomiSetu organizes land acquisition operations into three purpose-built stakeholder portals.

---

## 1. 🏛️ Government Portal

Designed for **District Magistrates (DM)**, **Competent Authorities for Land Acquisition (CALA)**, and **State/Central Ministry Administrators**.

### Key Responsibilities & Capabilities:
- **Project Gazetted Notification:** Upload and approve Section 3A (Intention to acquire), 3D (Declaration of acquisition), and Section 3G (Determination of compensation).
- **Land Parcel Adjudication:** Review conflicting ownership claims, encumbrance certificates, and heritage/forest clearances.
- **Compensation Sanctions:** Calculate and disburse compensation using formulaic multipliers mandated under the RFCTLARR Act (Market Value × Rural Multiplier + Solatium 100% + 12% Interest).
- **Public Grievance Redressal:** Review petitions, objections, and hearing schedules.

---

## 2. 🏗️ Acquiring Agency Portal

Designed for infrastructure project proponents such as **National Highways Authority of India (NHAI)**, **Ministry of Railways**, **Dedicated Freight Corridor Corporation (DFCCIL)**, and **Metro Rail Corporations**.

### Key Responsibilities & Capabilities:
- **Alignment Planning & GIS Upload:** Import KML / GeoJSON / Shapefiles representing corridor alignments.
- **Cadastral Overlay:** Automatically cross-reference survey numbers affected by proposed rights-of-way (RoW).
- **Joint Measurement Survey (JMS):** Log physical verification details, tree counts, structural assets, and borewells.
- **Escrow Fund Tracking:** Monitor deposited compensation reserves and track disbursement milestones.

---

## 3. 🌾 Landowner & Citizen Portal

Designed for **Farmers**, **Property Owners**, and **Affected Families (PAPs)**.

### Key Responsibilities & Capabilities:
- **Direct Aadhaar / Mobile OTP Authentication:** Effortless, passwordless citizen login.
- **Parcel Status Lookup:** Enter district, taluka, and survey number to check acquisition notification status.
- **Compensation Calculation Breakdown:** Transparent view of land valuation, structural asset appraisals, and solatium.
- **Digital Claims & Objections:** Submit title proofs, objection representations, and bank details for Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).
