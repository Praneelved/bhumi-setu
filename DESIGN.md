## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the authority, transparency, and precision required of apex state infrastructure. It balances legal gravity with digital modernity, eliminating bureaucratic visual clutter in favor of high-density clarity, deterministic status communication, and absolute legislative credibility.

### Personality & Emotional Response
- **Inviolable Authority:** Visual stability through strict alignment, architectural typography, and unambiguous structural lines.
- **Democratic Accessibility:** Radical clarity across varying digital literacy levels, harsh daylight operating conditions, and varied device viewports.
- **Civic Trust:** Zero decorative distraction; every element signifies statutory status, geographic demarcation, or fiscal disbursement accuracy.

### Design Movement
The style synthesizes **Civic Minimalism** with **High-Information Govtech Utility**. Drawing from classical institutional document standards and modern data-dense governance dashboards, the interface operates on pristine pure white canvases with precise Slate architectural grids, accented decisively with the tricolor palette: Navy/Ashoka Blue for sovereign command, Saffron for active legal notices and statutory alerts, and India Green for clearance validations and completed disbursements.

## Colors

The palette establishes an unmistakable civic identity rooted in national symbolism while adhering to rigorous WCAG 2.1 AAA contrast ratios across data-heavy administrative surfaces.

### Primary Role (Ashoka / Sovereign Navy)
- `#0A2540`: Primary authority color; utilized for primary action triggers, top-level administrative headers, sovereign emblems, and table key identifiers.
- `#00172A`: Deep Navy; used for high-emphasis text, key data readouts, and active state indicators.
- `#EBF3FA`: Subtle blue tint surface for selected parcel rows and active tab indicators.

### Secondary Role (India Green / Sanction & Yield)
- `#046A38`: Validation and environmental clearance; applied to completed disbursements, verified mutations, statutory approvals, and clean land parcel records.
- `#138808`: Bright green reserved for real-time GIS layer sync indicators and success metrics.
- `#EDF7EE`: Green-tinted container background for positive statutory determinations.

### Tertiary Role (Saffron / Statutory Notice & Urgency)
- `#E65100`: High-visibility administrative alert; used for pending gazette notifications (Section 11/19), compulsory acquisition notices, grievance escalation tags, and objection periods.
- `#FF671F`: Saffron focal accent for critical action highlights and map polygon overlays.
- `#FFF3EB`: Warm container fill for Section 15 hearing alerts and preliminary notifications.

### Neutral & System Foundations
- `#FFFFFF`: Pure white surface for primary operational cards, modals, and data grid cells.
- `#F8FAFC`: Crisp slate off-white canvas for system backgrounds and structural section divisions.
- `#F1F5F9`: Input container fill and muted toolbar background.
- `#E2E8F0`: Structural border color; provides razor-sharp boundary definition without visual noise.
- `#64748B`: Secondary metadata, cadastral reference numbers, and label typography.
- `#0F172A`: Primary text token; ensures optimal edge sharpness and legibility across standard screens.

## Typography

The type scale combines **Plus Jakarta Sans** for structural headers and numerical metrics with **Inter** for sustained administrative reading, legal declarations, and tabular cadastral data.

### Structural Rules
- **Tabular Figures:** All numeric displays (Survey Numbers, Khasra references, compensation values in INR ₹, and land areas in Hectares/Ares) must implement `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` to guarantee vertical baseline tracking across complex grids.
- **Statutory Codes:** Section identifiers (e.g., `RFCTLARR § 11(1)`, `Form 7/12`, `Khasra 412/1`) utilize `statutory-code` styling with uppercase tracking for instant identification against running text.
- **Hierarchical Discipline:** Headers avoid decorative weights; titles remain crisp, dark (`#0A2540` or `#0F172A`), and uncluttered.

## Layout & Spacing

The design system employs a deterministic 8px base rhythm (`space-xs` = 4px, `space-sm` = 8px, `space-base` = 16px) with a 12-column adaptive fluid grid engineered for dense analytical workstations and rugged field tablets.

### Layout Philosophy
- **Dashboard & Cadastral Workspace:** Max-width containers default to `1600px` for high-throughput multi-column inspection screens. Workspaces split between contextual data grids (7 or 8 columns) and GIS/Satellite split viewports (5 or 4 columns).
- **Form Factor Adaptations:**
  - **Desktop (≥1280px):** 12-column layout with 24px gutters and 32px safe margins. Persistent left rail for institutional navigation (State/District/Taluk selectors).
  - **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column layout with 16px gutters and 24px margins. Navigation collapses to an off-canvas drawer; dual-pane map/list stacks to a toggle view.
  - **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column layout with 12px gutters and 16px margins. Dense single-column feed optimized for thumb-reach site verification actions and camera upload modules.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects exaggerated, floaty drop shadows in favor of **Tonal Layering** backed by **Micro-Borders**. Shadows serve purely functional spatial indicators for sticky headers, elevated flyouts, and modal dialogs.

### Elevation Hierarchy
1. **Canvas Layer (Ground 0):** `#F8FAFC` base application plane.
2. **Surface Layer (Elevation 1):** `#FFFFFF` cards, data grid rows, and table surfaces, enclosed with a continuous `1px solid #E2E8F0` border. Zero blur shadow.
3. **Elevated Toolbar / Sticky Cadastral Nav (Elevation 2):** `#FFFFFF` with `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(10, 37, 64, 0.05), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(10, 37, 64, 0.03)`.
4. **Floating Map Controls & Popovers (Elevation 3):** `#FFFFFF` with `box-shadow: 0 4px 6px -1px rgba(10, 37, 64, 0.08), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(10, 37, 64, 0.06)`, framed by a crisp `#CBD5E1` border.
5. **Statutory Modals & Hearing Overlays (Elevation 4):** Anchored with a solid 40% `#0A2540` backdrop dim and `box-shadow: 0 20px 25px -5px rgba(10, 37, 64, 0.15)`.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a structured, architectural geometry (`roundedness: 1`). Radii stay tight and disciplined to maintain an institutional aesthetic and maximize tabular data density.

### Shape Values
- **Micro Radii (`rounded-sm` / 2px):** Badges, statutory act chips, map mini-tags, and dense table checkboxes.
- **Component Radii (`rounded` / 4px):** Standard buttons, text input fields, selection drop-downs, and table control panels.
- **Container Radii (`rounded-lg` / 8px):** Primary analytical cards, GIS viewport frames, and hearing schedule summary modules.
- **Outer Shell Radii (`rounded-xl` / 12px):** Administrative dialogs, high-level wizard containers, and top-tier KPI metric summaries.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Sovereign Action:** High-density `#0A2540` fill with pure white text, 4px corner radius, and minimum 40px touch height (`h-10 px-4`). Focus state indicates a strict 2px offset ring of `#0A2540`.
- **Statutory Alert Button:** `#E65100` fill with pure white text, designated solely for legal publishing triggers (e.g., "Issue Section 19 Declaration").
- **Secondary / Verification Action:** Transparent fill, `1px solid #CBD5E1` border, `#0A2540` text, transitioning to `#F1F5F9` on hover.
- **Clearance Affirmation:** `#046A38` fill with white text for final fund disbursement and award approval commands.

### Chips & Statutory Status Badges
- **RFCTLARR Notice Badge:** Pill format using `statutory-code` typography.
  - *Section 11 (Preliminary Notification):* Background `#FFF3EB`, text `#C2410C`, border `1px solid #FDBA74`.
  - *Section 19 (Declaration):* Background `#FEF3C7`, text `#B45309`, border `1px solid #FCD34D`.
  - *Award Declared (§ 23/30):* Background `#EDF7EE`, text `#046A38`, border `1px solid #86EFAC`.
  - *Litigation / Stay Order:* Background `#FEF2F2`, text `#B91C1C`, border `1px solid #FCA5A5`.
- **GIS Cadastral Tag:** `#F1F5F9` background, `#0F172A` bold text, coupled with an interactive icon indicating polygon verification status.

### Data Grids & Lists
- **Cell Compactness:** 48px baseline row height (`h-12`) on desktop, collapsible to 40px for high-density entry.
- **Alternating Logic:** Strict pure white `#FFFFFF` cells with bottom border `1px solid #E2E8F0`. Hover state triggers `#F8FAFC`.
- **Selected Land Record:** `#EBF3FA` with an active 3px `#0A2540` indicator on the cell's left vertical axis.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Fields:** Crisp `#FFFFFF` surface with `1px solid #CBD5E1` border. Active focus yields `1px solid #0A2540` with an accompanying subtle ring `0 0 0 1px #0A2540`.
- **Bilingual Label Pairs:** Primary label in bold English (`label-md`), paired with regional Indic text in neutral slate (`label-sm`) directly adjacent.
- **Validation Messages:** Unambiguous inline error/success text, reinforced with a 14px statutory icon to bypass reliance on color alone.

### Real-Time KPI Cards
- **Architecture:** `#FFFFFF` background encased in `1px solid #E2E8F0` with 8px radius.
- **Metrics Hierarchy:** Top accent micro-bar (2px) indicating workflow track (Navy for Total Hectares Acquired, Green for Direct Benefit Transfer Disbursed, Saffron for Pending Hearings). Big-figure numericals in `Plus Jakarta Sans` Bold (`28px`), accompanied by a percentage trend chip.

### GIS Map Overlays
- **Map Viewport Card:** Crisp 1px slate frame with rounded-lg edges. Floating control ribbons (Layers, Buffer Analysis, Measurement) styled with Elevation 3 parameters, pinned top-right.
- **Parcel Property Inspector:** Fixed slide-out drawer (360px) utilizing `surface-white`, displaying survey details, landowner hierarchy, encumbrance history, and Direct Benefit Transfer (PFMS) verification badges.
